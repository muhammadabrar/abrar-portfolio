---
title: Keeping full-stack delivery calm under pressure
description: A short operating style for remote product work—clear scopes, small ships, and systems that survive launch week.
date: 2024-11-04
---

The fastest way to lose a week is trying to polish everything before users touch anything.

## Scope in writing

A short brief beats a long meeting. Goals, constraints, and done-when criteria keep remote collaboration honest.

## Ship vertical slices

Prefer a thin path that works in production over disconnected frontend and backend milestones. Integration risk shows up early that way.

## Leave the system operable

Logs, seeds, deploy notes, and obvious admin paths matter as much as the feature demo. Launch week is quieter when the team can recover without you.
